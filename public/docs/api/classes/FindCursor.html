    Class FindCursor<TDocument>

    A lazy, batched cursor over the results of a find.

    Nothing is sent until the first read. Documents arrive in batches, and the cursor releases its server-side state when it is exhausted or closed. toArray() and for await close the cursor for you, even when the loop exits early or throws.
